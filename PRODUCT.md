# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Dispatchers, hospital staff, ambulance crews, and demo operators use the
console during a synthetic emergency coordination scenario. Dispatchers need
to scan eligibility, freshness, reasons, and next actions quickly. Other roles
need focused, permission-aware workflows.

## Product Purpose

Conclave coordinates a simulated emergency from incident intake through
ambulance selection, hospital acceptance, resource reservation, routing, and
mission progression. Success means the operator can understand why a choice was
made and see when data is stale, simulated, rejected, or unavailable.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, operational. The interface should feel like a trusted command
console under pressure, not a consumer app or a marketing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS cards, purple gradients, decorative glass panels, oversized
landing-page hero sections, emoji status icons, and dense tables that hide the
next action.

## Design Principles

- Put the next safe operational decision in the strongest visual position.
- Explain eligibility and exclusion in the same view as the recommendation.
- Treat freshness, simulation, and unknown capacity as first-class states.
- Use restrained motion and clear status language to reduce operator load.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA behavior: visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, live
regions for asynchronous state, status text in addition to color, comfortable
touch targets, responsive reflow, and reduced-motion support.
